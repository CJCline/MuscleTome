# Privacy Policy for MuscleTome

**Last Updated:** February 22, 2025

MuscleTome ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your data.

## 1. Local-First Architecture
MuscleTome is a local-first exercise logging application designed to help users track workouts and fitness routines on their device.

## 2. Data Storage
All workout data, exercise history, logs, and user preferences are stored **locally on your device**. No personal workout data or user information is uploaded to cloud databases or remote servers.

## 3. Data Collection, Tracking, and Transmission
- **No Personal Data Collection:** MuscleTome does not collect, harvest, or track personal user data, location info, or device identifiers.
- **No External Transmission:** The application does not transmit your personal data or workout logs to external servers, advertisers, or third-party tracking services.

## 4. Third-Party Services
MuscleTome does not integrate third-party analytics, behavioral tracking, or advertising SDKs.

## 5. User Data Rights & Deletion
Because all data resides exclusively on your local device, you retain complete ownership and control over your data:
- You can clear or delete your data anytime via Android App Settings.
- Uninstalling the MuscleTome app will remove all locally stored application data.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
